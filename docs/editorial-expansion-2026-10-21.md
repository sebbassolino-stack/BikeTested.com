## Source and voice decisions

- Sensor mechanism: Aventon technical explainer. Avoid its broad claims that all torque systems are more efficient, or that all cadence systems feel alike. Explain controller tuning separately.
- Motors and shifting: Bosch drive-unit and eShift documentation. Avoid ranking power labels across unlike systems.
- Belt care: Gates manufacturer reference. Avoid the marketing claim “maintenance-free”; retain cleaning, tension/alignment, and compatible-frame considerations.
- Chain service and valves: Park Tool repair instructions; REI pump guidance. Use an independent sequence and emphasize the correct drivetrain/pump instructions.
- Pressure: SRAM/Zipp pressure calculator and wheel manual. Schwalbe pages returned maintenance responses and were not used as evidence. No universal PSI chart; do not tell riders to inflate every tire to its printed maximum.
- Range: Bosch range assistant and battery guide. Clearly distinguish nominal battery energy, motor power, estimates, and measured results.
- Charging cost: original worked arithmetic using explicitly hypothetical inputs, plus the Bosch battery guide and CPSC charger guidance. No current tariff or household-savings claim.
- Tire types and luggage: REI equipment guides. Frame access: Aventon. Folding dimensions and handling: Tern. Recommendations are Bike Tested editorial applications of those facts, not invented riding tests.

The source links appear on each new article and in canonical guide data. New article publish/modify dates are October 21. Existing substantive article copy retains its October 19 dates.

## Discoverability and conversion

- A direct answer, searchable title, unique description, table of contents, per-article dates, organization author link, Article/Breadcrumb structured data.
- Twelve visible three-question FAQ sets with matching FAQPage data. This is machine-readable structure, not a claim of Google FAQ rich-result eligibility.
- Tables or practical steps where they answer the question, with captions and keyboard-accessible overflow regions.
- Curated guide-to-guide links, category hub discovery, homepage features, and reciprocal links from relevant reviews.
- Guide hub grouped into choosing, e-bike systems, maintenance and everyday gear; every guide has one hub placement.
- Pagefind rebuild, generated llms.txt, guide entries in agent.json, and real guide lastmod values in sitemap.
- No build-time IndexNow calls or production side effects. GitHub main triggers Cloudflare normally after validation.
