## Product facts and buyer-facing limits

The official listing gives a 1,100W peak single motor, 624Wh 48V 13Ah battery, up to 44-mile claimed range, up to 28 mph, 20% climbing grade, adjustable dual swingarm suspension, 10 × 2.5-inch tubeless tires, E-ABS with front and rear mechanical disc brakes, 61.5 lb weight, 287 lb maximum load, and folded dimensions of 49 × 28.3 × 21.3 inches. These are manufacturer specifications, not Bike Tested ride measurements. The 44-mile maximum is conditioned on a 165 lb load, single-motor mode, constant 9.3 mph, moderate temperature, low wind, and flat, dry pavement.

The product page footer says “For use on private property only,” despite commute-oriented marketing. The manual separately tells owners to check local road rules and required equipment. The manual also advises avoiding puddles, wet ground, rain, storms, and snow, and warns that wet surfaces increase braking distance despite the IP54 listing. The product page advertises a two-year worry-free warranty; the Gosoul 2 manual's component chart gives one year for the frame, motor, controller, throttle, and display, six months for the battery, charger, disc brake and folding assembly, 30 days for the wheel, and 14 days for grips and other parts. Confirm written warranty coverage for the exact purchase.
