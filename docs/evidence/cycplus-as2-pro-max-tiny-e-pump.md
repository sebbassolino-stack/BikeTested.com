## Product facts and buyer-facing limits

The official product image titled “Specifications” and the AS2 Pro Max manual list a 120 PSI maximum, 205 g weight and 81 × 60 × 32 mm size. The manual lists a 600 mAh, 11.1 V battery (6.66 Wh), USB-C charging, a live pressure display, adjustable target, automatic shutoff, a silicone case, hose, Presta converter, seal part, charging cable and ball needle. CYCPLUS markets a 1 PSI high-precision gauge and one-hour full charge. The product graphic lists a USB-C 5 V 1.5 A input while the manual lists 5 V 2 A and 9 V 1.5 A; charge-time and charger wording stays qualified.

The manual describes direct Presta setup, a reversible nozzle insert for Schrader, and use of the included hose for Schrader. It says to use the hose above 100 PSI to reduce air leakage and to always use the hose with TPU inner tubes using resin valves because direct heat may melt the valve. It warns that the valve can be hot after inflation. The pump stops after three minutes of continuous inflation, stops when it overheats, and should cool before use resumes. The manual lists 10 minutes before restarting after an over-temperature warning.

The manual’s full-charge reference data gives 700 × 25 tire fills from 0 to 80 PSI in 40–50 seconds (8 inflations), 0–110 PSI in 65–75 seconds (5), and 0–120 PSI in 75–85 seconds (4). For a 700 × 28 tire it lists 0–80 PSI in 45–55 seconds (7), 0–110 PSI in 90–100 seconds (4), and 0–120 PSI in 95–105 seconds (3). For a 29 × 2.2 tire it lists 0–50 PSI in 130–140 seconds (3). CYCPLUS labels these experimental data for reference only; they are not Bike Tested measurements.

The product page describes a splashproof pouch but does not state an IP water-resistance rating for the pump. CYCPLUS’s current limited warranty gives all other products a two-year period and excludes water ingress, unauthorized repair, misuse and other non-normal use. No pump waterproofing claim is made.
