## Manufacturer-listed specifications

The user manual’s Specifications section lists 65 × 47.5 × 28 mm; 87 g; a 400 mAh, 7.4 V, 2.96 Wh battery; USB Type-C input of 5 V / 2 A or 7 V / 1.5 A; 2.5–7.5 A operating current; and 120 PSI maximum pressure. The controls display live pressure and battery level, switch between PSI and BAR, accept a target pressure, and stop inflation automatically at the target.

The manual shows the nozzle set up for Presta and reversed for Schrader. It recommends the hose for Schrader and instructs riders to use the hose above 100 PSI to prevent nozzle leakage. It specifically warns that TPU inner tubes with resin valves require the hose to prevent overheating; the valve may be hot after inflation. The packing list shows the inflator, silicone case, waterproof bag, spare seal part, hose, ball needle, Presta valve converter, and cable.

The manual’s full-charge “Working performance” table is labeled experimental reference data. Examples used in the review:
