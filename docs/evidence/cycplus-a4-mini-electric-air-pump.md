## Product information

The official A4 gallery lists a 120 PSI maximum, ±1 PSI pressure accuracy, 0.46 lb weight, dimensions of about 4.02 × 1.97 × 1.77 inches, a 2,000mAh battery and an integrated hose that rotates 180 degrees. A gallery image advertises up to 19 fills of a 700 × 25c tire per charge but does not state start pressure, end pressure or test conditions. These remain manufacturer claims.

The product package image shows an inflator, charging cable and Presta valve converter. CYCPLUS’s pump FAQ says a Schrader tire needs no additional attachment and Presta inflation requires the valve adapter. The official FAQ groups A4 with A2/A3/A5 for its battery and one-charge car-tire claim; the review does not extend that claim into a guaranteed result. The current CYCPLUS public instruction-manual directory lists A3 Mini but not A4 Mini. Public A4 materials checked here do not state a charging interface or recharge time.
