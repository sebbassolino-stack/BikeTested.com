## Manufacturer specifications and conflicts

- Tomofree lists a 750W nominal brushless hub motor and 70 N·m torque, 20 × 4-inch tires, an adjustable front fork, rear spring suspension, dual mechanical disc brakes, full-twist throttle, integrated lighting, and a removable 48V 20Ah battery.
- The Shopify description lists full-twist throttle, five-level PAS and four riding modes. The product page does not explain how these controls/settings relate or identify the PAS sensor type or gearing.
- Range is listed as 20–40 miles in page highlights/specifications; the Shopify product description says 30 miles. Both remain seller claims.
- Speed is listed as 20/28 mph in page highlights/specifications, while the motor section says 25 mph. The page labels the bike “Class 2/3” and “street-legal”; no legal classification is independently asserted.
- The page suggests rider heights of 5–6 ft; the Shopify description says 154–184 cm. The geometry section lists a 34.6-inch seat height and 38.5-inch handlebar-to-seat distance, but no standover figure. Overall length appears as both 63.3 and 63.6 inches.
- The specification page lists the e-bike at 84.7 lb and separately lists the battery at 9.92 lb; it does not clearly say whether the bike weight includes the battery. Listed maximum load is 330 lb. The product page says a user manual is included, but the public manuals page does not list an R700 manual.
- The page lists a 54.6V, 3.0A charger and 6–7 hour charge time.
- Tomofree's warranty page lists one year for the battery and motor, three months for the display, and one month for tires and several other listed parts. The return policy allows 30-day change-of-mind returns with a 12% restocking fee and customer-paid shipping. Confirm the current terms for the exact order.
