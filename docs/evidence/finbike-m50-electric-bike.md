## Manufacturer facts and conflicts

- The product page lists 27.5 × 2.3-inch tires, a 250W motor with 500W peak, a 36V 13Ah (468Wh) battery, 62–70km range, 25km/h maximum speed with an unlock claim to 32km/h, and 150kg maximum load.
- The linked M50 manual lists 28kg bike weight, 1815 × 700 × 1120mm dimensions, 250W motor power, a 36V 13Ah battery, 88+km laboratory reference range, 3–4 hour charging time, 42V 3A charger output, and a one-year limited warranty. Its range note says conditions such as load, humidity, wind, and riding habits affect distance.
- FinBike's product page conflicts with its linked manual on range. The page lists 62–70km; the manual lists 88+km and labels the figure a laboratory reference. We do not report either as a Bike Tested measurement.
- The product page's main safety text says not to operate the bike in wet conditions, while its FAQ says light rain is acceptable. The FAQ gives a one-year main-component warranty and 14-day returns, but badges on the same page say two-year warranty and 30-day returns; the manual lists one-year limited warranty. Ask FinBike for written, destination-specific terms.
- The seller FAQ recommends 5 ft 3 in–6 ft 3 in rider height, but there is no geometry chart on the listing. The FAQ question calls the bike an “isinwheel M50,” suggesting the model-specific wording may not be reliable enough to establish fit.
- The product page markets the model as UKCA/CE-certified and road legal in some regions. Those remain seller statements. We make no independent certification or legal classification claim; confirm local requirements for the configured speed and intended routes.
- The package list includes the bike, charger, manual, safety instructions, two battery keys, bicycle pump, front and rear fenders, and tools.
