## Manufacturer facts used in the review

- COOSPO describes the BC107 as an entry-level GPS computer with GPS + BeiDou positioning.
- The product page says it supports ANT+ heart-rate, speed, cadence, and power sensors, and does not support Bluetooth sensor connections.
- COOSPO says the CoospoRide app supports setup, ride-data sync, and firmware updates. It says .FIT activity can sync to Strava through the app and that the device cannot export .FIT files over USB.
- COOSPO lists up to 28 hours with GPS enabled and says actual use varies with personal use habits. This is a manufacturer estimate, not an independent runtime result.
- The manufacturer page lists IP67, 64 g, 80 × 54 × 20.64 mm, a 2-hour charge time, a USB cable, and a basic mount.
- Conflicting screen-size values are omitted from the public review; its buying advice notes only that COOSPO's pages disagree. The product-page parameter section says 2.3-inch FSTN; COOSPO's product comparison says 2.4-inch FSTN, while the product heading/image alt also says 2.4-inch LCD. Verify with the seller if this detail is decisive.
