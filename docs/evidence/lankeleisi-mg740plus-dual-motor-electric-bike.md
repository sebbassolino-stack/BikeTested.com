## Manufacturer specifications used

- Two 48V 1,000W brushless hub motors; the feature section says 95Nm maximum torque while the specification table says 90Nm.
- Removable 48V 20Ah Samsung battery using 18650 cells; 960Wh calculated from nominal voltage and amp-hours.
- Manufacturer range: 60–70km pure electric and 120–150km pedal assist in the specification table; the feature copy says up to 70km and 150km, while a separate highlight says 70–130km per charge. The specification table says its figures come from a 60kg load, 25°C and 20km/h average on a flat road.
- 45–51km/h seller-listed maximum-speed claim; the US product page does not state a bicycle class.
- 12-point Hall speed sensor, color LCD, seven-speed Shimano drivetrain, DYISLAND hydraulic disc brakes and 26 × 4.0-inch Chaoyang tires.
- Product copy says full suspension; the specification table specifies a 100mm lockable front fork but does not detail rear suspension travel or setup.
- Listed bike weight 34kg / 74.95lb. Geometry chart: recommended rider height 170–195cm, saddle height 31.4–37.4in, standover 27.9in, wheelbase 46in, overall length 74in, handlebar width 28.3in, and 180kg maximum load. Specification table expresses the load as 400lb.
- Warranty terms page, last updated September 2023: one year for specified components, two years for motors and battery, three years for frame; original-owner limitation and exclusions apply. Read the current terms before purchase.
