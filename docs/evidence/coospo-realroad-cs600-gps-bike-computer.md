## Manufacturer facts used in the review

- COOSPO lists a 2.4-inch color transflective LCD touchscreen, offline maps, 8 GB storage, five positioning systems (GPS, BDS, QZSS, GLONASS, Galileo), and a 69 g weight.
- The product page lists 84 × 53 × 18 mm dimensions, a Type-C charging port, a two-hour charge time, and IPX7.
- COOSPO claims up to 36 hours with GPS enabled. This is a manufacturer claim, not a Bike Tested runtime result.
- The product page says the CS600 connects to compatible speed, cadence, heart-rate, and power sensors, and lists smart trainers, electronic shifters, radar tail lights, e-bikes, and smart bike lights.
- COOSPO's navigation FAQ says route navigation requires a GPX file. It describes converting ride history on the device, importing a file from third-party apps through CoospoRide, or copying one to the Course folder over USB.
- The listed standalone package contains the CS600 main unit, Type-C cable, basic mount, screen protector, user manual, two rubber rings, rubber mat, and anti-lost rope.
